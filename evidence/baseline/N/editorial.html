The Coast at Low Tide

Mara Vega · Field report · 7 min read

Read the feature

At low tide the boats sit crooked in the mud. From the sea wall you can see the dark line the water left on each hull. On the steps below, a cook rinses grit from the hinges of a basket of clams.

In the kitchen the first sound is the tap of shells against enamel. Then the lid goes on. A little later, lifting it releases a smell so close to the wet steps that for a moment the two places feel like one.

The evening fish market

As the light fades, the last trays of silver fish move from the ice to the scales, then into paper parcels.

Recipe
Lemon broth

Simmer two strips of lemon zest in two cups of vegetable stock for five minutes. Remove the zest, add lemon juice to taste and serve hot.
