The Coast at Low Tide

The water retreats, leaving a second shore beneath the first.

At the foot of the slipway a ribbon of weed marks the height of last night’s tide. Below it the stones are glossy and dark. Turn one over and a little crab folds itself into the mud.

Back at the stove, the last of the cooking liquor sits in the bottom of the pot. I let the grit settle before pouring it off. A strip of lemon peel lifts in the steam.
