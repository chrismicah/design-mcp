## S3

**Goree, Doosti, Crandall & Su. Investigating the Homogenization of Web Design (CHI 2021)**

Longitudinal website-image analysis (2003–2019) and 11 professional interviews. The authors report a decline of over 30% in average layout distance since 2007; source/library overlap, standard palettes and mobile support appear in the discussion. Applicable as a pre-generative baseline. Abstract-level read.

**Entezami & Endres. Biased or Personalized? The Impact of Personal Information on AI-driven Development**

## S10

**Brown et al. and Holtzman et al. on language modeling and decoding**

GPT-3 is described as autoregressive. Holtzman et al. compared decoding strategies with language models held fixed and found differences in generated text repetition and quality. Supports distinguishing the conditional model distribution from the algorithm choosing a sequence. Neither study measured a UI or a particular CSS default.
