# Creative website reviews, November 22, 2026

The most useful question for a new site is what a visitor should be able to notice or do that a conventional layout would make harder. Choose that one moment before adopting a visual device. Review the working interaction at desktop and phone widths.

## Fieldglass experiment

Compare two Smithsonian photographs of meteoritic iron early on the page. Check identities and accession numbers against the [Pitts record](https://naturalhistory.si.edu/object/nmnhmineralsciences_1014156) and the [Harriman record](https://naturalhistory.si.edu/object/nmnhmineralsciences_1021031). If a visual effect draws attention away from the specimen's actual texture, change the mechanism.

- **Single plate.** The initial design emphasizes one photograph beside an oversized title. The first eligible blind ballot scored 8.30 at both widths. The critic wanted larger mobile photography, and the owner objected to the warm-paper treatment and type.
- **Projection blue.** The alternate typography and stronger cool color scored 7.70 at both widths. The animation left an initially narrow strip of the new photograph while the caption already described the new state.
- **Shared seam.** The next plan moves both etched photographs into the opening view. A short shift makes the selected specimen larger while preserving both photographs. This tests whether the scene supports a direct comparison at phone size.
- **Tracing desk.** A separate research agent proposed tracing the bands. The local experiment uses a visible button to show a feature directly over a real photo. The visitor can inspect the photo at its normal size and remove the annotation.
- **Fixed inspection window.** Planning compared three mechanisms. An instant swap keeps two etched photographs in the same window, a specimen notebook collects two crops at their source coordinates, and an optional identification challenge asks a visitor to match a mystery crop. The first trial uses the instant swap because a phone visitor can compare full-size evidence in place. Test whether it preserves the photo’s position, shows the selected name and record, and retains the full view. A visitor failing to distinguish the dark inclusion from the crossing bands would rule out the approach.

For each round, pin the browser, assets and task. Record image dimensions and decoded status, exact loaded type, layout and key states. Give a fresh reviewer the anonymous pages and a task they can finish by keyboard or touch. Preserve the archive that establishes what the reviewer saw. Count only audited scores. Keep the best verified result and strengthen the same idea while it improves. Pivot when it stops. An independent second opinion and 9.5 at both widths remain separate release gates.
