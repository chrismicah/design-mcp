# Named editable result holdout

A shopper for a first counseling appointment should see which counselor
will answer and when they can call. They can prepare a private note that
opens the conversation. Keep it calm and direct. Omit portraits, pricing,
treatment guarantees, and booking or submission. Keep the earlier
consultation palette and typography fixed between arms. The primary
composition is contact facts followed by the short form and its one result.
Our one experimental change concerns where focus lands after preview.

Compare exact source and freshly installed control and treatment replies.
Only `direction.action.instruction` may change in the consultation reply.
Freeze a Madison counseling brief before dispatch. Protect the explicit
form override and the full replies for the 48 existing unrelated briefs.
Generate both arms with the same brief, model, effort and prompt prefix.
The trial must focus the named editor immediately, keep the visible
editable box and local-only message in the first frame, and preserve every
other viewport and keyboard check at 320, 390 and 1440 px. Keep the original
Dayton repair as authored evidence. Neither branch earns release
promotion without a matched improvement and independent visual review.

W3C’s focus-order guidance calls for a logical, usable navigation
sequence and allows programmatic focus on meaningful static targets.
The selected direct editor is a choice for this editable result, with
a heading for read-only previews. It is not a general WCAG requirement.
The existing Reddit clinic critique motivates accurate action wording and
stable contact details. X’s replay report motivates retaining exact
artifact identities and measuring the outcome independently.
