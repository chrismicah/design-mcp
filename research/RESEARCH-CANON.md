# Design canon applied to screens

| Reading | Principle to carry forward | Application and check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Don Norman, *The Design of Everyday Things*, author overview and public chapter outline | Signifiers help people discover possible actions. Feedback closes the gap between action and result. | Use action labels with an object and predictable focus. Include pending, success, empty and recoverable error states. Check a novice can predict what the primary control does and recognize the result. [Author’s overview](https://jnd.org/books/the-design-of-everyday-things-revised-and-expanded-edition/) |
| Josef Müller-Brockmann, *Grid Systems in Graphic Design*, publisher synopsis | Reusable alignments organize multiple kinds of information across a composition. | Specify alignment anchors, spans and reading order. Break one axis for the subject where appropriate and restore normal flow at narrow widths. Check that the break survives a squint test while content order remains intact at 390 px. [Niggli](https://niggli.ch/en/products/rastersysteme-fur-die-visuelle-gestaltung) |
| Dieter Rams, ten principles as presented by Vitsœ | Usefulness, comprehensibility, honesty, detail and economy. | Use surfaces and separators to describe layering and relationships. Show real product evidence instead of invented numbers or decorative credibility claims. Remove styling that adds choices without clarifying content. [Vitsœ](https://www.vitsoe.com/us/about/good-design) |
| Robert Bringhurst via Richard Rutter’s publicly available adaptation, §2.1.2 | Comfortable measure depends on type and medium. Rutter translates print recommendations to fluid browser sizes. | Declare a long-form max measure in relative units, use a fluid heading/body relationship, and recheck wrapping with loaded fonts and longer words. Use a type scale to communicate information levels and check optical sizes at the target width. [Public excerpt and web application](https://webtypography.net/2.1.2) |
| Francis D. K. Ching and Corky Binggeli, *Interior Design Illustrated*, 4th ed., public limited preview | Coordinate function, spatial enclosure, visual elements and material decisions as one system. | Treat outer margin, content grouping and the focal object as related proportions. Use repeatable gutters and a few intentionally expanded gaps for rhythm. A background material or surface gets a functional or brand-specific job. Check the empty space around actual content and how the continuity survives mobile. [Limited preview](https://books.google.com/books?vid=ISBN9781119377207) |

Proportion, tracking and duration ranges in the project brief are house acceptance targets. The readings support reasoning about relationships and constraints, with no universal requirement for any one numerical value. Retain familiar affordances in task-critical controls and give editorial freedom to composition, type and imagery. Discard print column counts as universal defaults, furniture dimensions, building-code details, and any claim that decorative material makes digital surfaces more trustworthy. Public excerpts and descriptions were used where full books were unavailable.
